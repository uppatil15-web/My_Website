Background & Vision

Driven by Optimism & Purpose

While my professional days are spent turning messy operational systems into clean, functional digital products, my broader mindset is shaped by continuous learning—whether that is diving into behavioral psychology, experimenting with new software prototypes, or dissecting how everyday things work. Beyond the screen, you will likely find me brewing a thoughtful cup of tea or playing an old bollywood piece!
