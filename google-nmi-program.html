Google NMI Program

Helping a Non-Profit Montessori School push towards their goal.

Google Non-Profit Marketing Immersion (NMI) is an excellent hands-on learning opportunity to apply digital marketing skills to help a non-profit organization thrive.

What was the challenge?

We worked with a Non-Profit Montessori school in Greenville, South Carolina. They wanted to improve their enrollment and drive community donations.

What we did

Together with 3 colleagues, I analyzed current marketing efforts, website content, and past Google Ads campaign data. We pivoted the strategy from maximizing conversion events to maximizing targeted impression reach and click volume for local awareness.

We identified key technical website improvements (missing meta descriptions, broken CTAs, backlink structure) and engineered a localized 8-week campaign keyword strategy.

Result

Delivered a comprehensive digital marketing roadmap for the Montessori school, boosting ad engagement, impression share, and actionable site traffic.
