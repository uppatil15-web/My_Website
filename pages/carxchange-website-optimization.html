CarXchange Website Optimization

"Getting a quality website is not an expense but rather an investment" — Dr. Christopher Dayagdag

Imagine you're a product manager at an Auto-Retail company called CarXchange. On a fine Monday morning, you gather with your teammates for the weekly meeting. The news? Sales have been sluggish for months, and operating costs are high. Our expert data analyst shows that car models are oversupplied across certain regions.

A team of 4 product managers take the lead on solving the problem (we call ourselves the CarBears 😉). We start brainstorming to define the problem. We realize that inventory cost is the major driver for the bottom-line, leading to an exciting journey of extracting data-driven insights, performing website optimization, and designing A/B testing strategies with a potential overall inventory reduction of 20%!
