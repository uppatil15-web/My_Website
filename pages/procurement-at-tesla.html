Supply Chain at Tesla

An exciting deep dive into a supply chain adventure

How does one manage a supply chain at Tesla? That's the question everyone on my team was trying to answer during my time at Tesla. With a fast-paced, ever-changing, and challenging environment, working in Tesla's Indirect Procurement team has been nothing short of enlightening.

The specialty of this particular experience was that I was thrown in the fire and left to figure out stuff for myself, which was extremely rewarding. I got to realize the potential of being a self-sustained owner of something.

Procurement is like managing a small business. You get to look into every aspect of a commodity, making you a complete business professional. Working with suppliers to ensure supply at the right time, in the right cost, in the right quantity; working with stakeholders to ensure their expectations were fulfilled; communicating results to leadership in high-visibility projects; and developing my own leadership and storytelling capabilities while managing everything was the main takeaway for me.
